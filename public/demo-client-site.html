Third-Party Client Website Example

Autonomous Voice Help Desk Integration

This is an example of an external website where a client has embedded your AI Voice Help Desk agent.

How it works for your clients:

The client only needed to add one line of code to their website before </body>: 

<script src="./widget.js" data-agent-id="YOUR_AGENT_ID" defer></script>

Try it out!

Look at the bottom-right corner of this page. You will see the floating Voice Help Desk launcher button.

Click on it to expand the voice panel, then tap the microphone orb to speak with the assistant in real-time!
